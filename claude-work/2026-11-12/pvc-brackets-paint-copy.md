## What customers are told today (from the email thread RE: 03-4216598/Div 5)

- The product page says the bracket comes pre-primed (per Rachel Cross, CX, Nov 11).
- The product overview says: "It's lightweight, easy to cut and install, and can be painted or left in its natural
  white finish." (quoted by Rachel)
- The install instructions say painting is highly recommended (per Rachel).
- Product Info (Ryan Shumaker, Nov 10): "we sell this product as unfinished millwork (we did not paint these) and they
  should be primed and painted before install." Milled edges can show some roughness; a light-grit sanding helps, then
  prime and paint.

So the overview line ("or left in its natural white finish") is the one that conflicts with the instructions and CX.

before the copy goes live. The draft below uses Ryan's wording (unfinished; prime and paint) and has the pre-primed
