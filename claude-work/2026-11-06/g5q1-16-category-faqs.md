## 1. Real Wood Beams (/wood-beams)

**Are these beams real wood?**
Yes. The beams on this page are AmeriCraft real wood beams. If you want the look of wood in a lighter material, see Faux Wood Beams.

**What woods and textures can I choose?**
AmeriCraft beams come in 4 textures, and White Oak is offered in all 4. Each product page lists the species and finishes for that beam.

**Are they solid beams?**
No. AmeriCraft beams are 3-sided U-beams, a hollow box shape that wraps around a ceiling or a support you already have.

**Can I use them outdoors?**
No. AmeriCraft real wood beams are for interior use only.

**Can I see the wood before I order?**
Yes. AmeriCraft sample kits are on this page and in Beam Samples & Touch-Up Kits.

**How long does shipping take?**
It depends on the size. Each product page shows the lead time for the size you pick.

## 3. Faux Metal Beams (/faux-metal-beams)

They are ForgeCraft faux steel beams: decorative beams that give a room the look of steel.

**Where are the faux wood beams?**
They have their own page: Faux Wood Beams. For a real wood look, see Real Wood Beams.

## 4. Fireplaces (/fireplaces)

**What does this section include?**
Complete fireplace surrounds, wood mantels, MDF mantels and faux wood beam mantels, plus mantel shelves and the parts that go with them.

**What's the difference between a mantel shelf and a surround?**
A mantel shelf is the shelf above the fireplace. A surround frames the whole opening, with legs and a header.

**Can a beam mantel be used as a floating shelf?**
Yes. The Heritage Timber beam mantel is sold for use as a mantel or as a floating shelf.

**Do you sell corbels and brackets for mantels?**
Yes. AD carries corbels and brackets in mantel depths. Rustic Wood Corbels is a good place to start.

**How do I install a beam mantel?**
Watch the mantel install video on this page, and follow the install guide linked on each product page.

Follow the install guide on your beam or mantel's product page. It covers the install steps for that product.
