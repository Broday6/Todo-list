## Standing rules every automation follows

- Draft, never send. Brody approves every email and Teams message.
- Read-only in Google Ads, Microsoft Ads, NetSuite (Preview, never Save) and Miva.
- Never mark a board task done. Never invent tasks or meetings.
- Never judge ads on ROAS. Judge portal groups on spend against the 7.5% cap and 12% growth.
- Push to Brody's phone only when something needs him.
